# Tuti Lab Research Journal

This directory publishes selected research notes from the Tuti project. We share methods, synthetic examples, observations, limitations, and questions that can benefit from external review.

## Publication rules

- Publish only material reviewed for privacy and security.
- Use synthetic or sanitized examples; never publish guest records, private conversations, credentials, or operational logs.
- Distinguish observed results from hypotheses and proposals.
- Include test conditions and limitations with every metric.
- Do not present a small internal experiment as a general benchmark.
